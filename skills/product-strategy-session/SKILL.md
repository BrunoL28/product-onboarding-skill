## Purpose
Guide PMs through a comprehensive product strategy session by orchestrating positioning, problem framing, customer discovery, and roadmap planning into a cohesive end-to-end process—moving from vague direction to concrete, validated strategy with clear positioning, target customers, problem statements, and a prioritized roadmap, aligned across stakeholders before committing to execution.

A repeatable process for establishing or refreshing strategy, typically spanning 2–4 weeks with multiple touchpoints and explicit decision points.

## Phases (with decision points)
1. **Positioning & market context** — positioning workshop, `proto-persona`, jobs-to-be-done. Decision: enough customer context? If no, run discovery.
2. **Problem framing & validation** — problem-framing canvas, `problem-statement`, optional journey map. Decision: problem validated? If no, run discovery interviews.
3. **Solution exploration** — opportunity solution tree or `lean-ux-canvas`; `epic-hypothesis`; optional story map. Decision: test solutions before committing?
4. **Prioritization & roadmap** — `prioritization-advisor`; score & sequence into releases; optional market sizing.
5. **Stakeholder alignment** — optional press release; present strategy; refine on feedback.
6. **Execution planning** — `epic-breakdown-advisor`; `user-story`; plan first sprint/release.

### Anti-Patterns
Not a feature brainstorm; not waterfall planning; not a solo PM exercise.

## Common Pitfalls
1. Skipping problem validation.
2. Solo PM exercise (include cross-functional participants).
3. No executive sponsorship.
4. Running all phases regardless (use decision points to adapt).
5. Strategy session becomes a permanent process → time-box to 2–4 weeks, then execute.

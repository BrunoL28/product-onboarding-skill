## Purpose
Guide PMs through strategic roadmap planning by orchestrating prioritization, epic definition, stakeholder alignment, and release sequencing into a structured process—moving from disconnected feature requests to a cohesive, outcome-driven roadmap. Not a Gantt chart: a strategic communication tool showing what you're building, why it matters, and how it ladders to business outcomes.

## Types of roadmaps
- **Now/Next/Later** (agile, uncertainty).
- **Theme-based** (exec communication).
- **Timeline/quarters** (resource planning).
- Avoid the feature-list anti-pattern (no strategic narrative).

## Application (phases)
1. **Gather inputs:** business goals/OKRs, validated customer problems, technical constraints, stakeholder requests.
2. **Define initiatives (epics):** use `epic-hypothesis`; T-shirt effort estimates; map each epic to a business outcome.
3. **Prioritize:** choose a framework (RICE/ICE/Value-Effort); score; adjust for strategic fit.
4. **Sequence:** map dependencies; organize into Now/Next/Later or quarters with a walking skeleton first; validate capacity with engineering.
5. **Communicate:** strategic narrative, outcome focus, flexibility ("plan, not contract"); gather feedback; refine; publish.

## Common Pitfalls
1. Feature-driven roadmap (no outcomes).
2. Prioritizing by HiPPO → use a transparent framework.
3. Roadmap as commitment (waterfall) → communicate as a plan subject to learning.
4. No dependencies mapped.
5. Solo-PM roadmap → gather inputs and present drafts for feedback.
